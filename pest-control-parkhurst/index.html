Parkhurst is on Rockhampton's northern edge, with newer housing and larger blocks. Keep landscaping, mulch and garden beds away from the slab edge so it stays dry and visible for inspection.

Termites

Rockhampton's hot, humid summers and wet season from December to March suit termites, and Queensland also has drywood termites that live inside timber. Have the home inspected regularly and check for mud tubes, hollow timber and damp areas. Read the Rockhampton termite guide and what to expect from an inspection.

Licensing

In Queensland, pest management technicians need a pest management licence from Queensland Health under the Medicines and Poisons Act 2019. The licence lists what the holder is authorised to do, such as urban pests or timber pests. Chemical termite work also needs a Termite Management—Chemical licence from the QBCC.
